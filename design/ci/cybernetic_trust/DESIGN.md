## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-octane PC gaming engineering merged with rigorous institutional trust. It bridges the gap between enthusiast tech culture and enterprise-grade hardware inspection. The visual posture is precise, dark-mode-first, and highly kinetic, utilizing electric neon lime accents to cut through deep, nocturnal slate backdrops.

### Design Tone & Values
- **Engineered Confidence:** High-precision data display, crisp metric hierarchies, and transparent condition reports reassure buyers about pre-owned electronics.
- **Enthusiast Energy:** Tactical neon glows, high-contrast dark surfaces, and subtle angular touches honor the gaming rig and custom-PC community.
- **Functional Clarity:** Dense hardware specifications, benchmarks, and multi-currency pricing (notably Thai Baht ฿) remain effortlessly readable without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep dark mode, relying on an obsidian canvas that makes phosphor-green and electric lime illuminate key interactive pathways and trust markers.

### Primary Accents
- `primary`: `#bbf246` (Electric Lime) — The hero driver for critical actions, key tags, active tabs, and primary calls to action.
- `primary-glow`: `rgba(187, 242, 70, 0.25)` — Ambient halo used behind major interactive items and hardware hero visualizers.

### Supporting & Functional Colors
- `secondary`: `#22c55e` (Verified Green) — Utilized strictly for passed inspection markers, guaranteed condition tags, and authentic seller credentials.
- `tertiary`: `#facc15` (Diagnostic Gold) — Reserved for hardware star ratings, exclusive discount tags, and warning callouts.
- `danger`: `#ef4444` (Telemetry Red) — Depletes stock alerts, critical error thresholds, or hardware flags.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- `bg-canvas`: `#0a0d14` — Deepest space background.
- `surface-base`: `#111622` — Default card background and lower modules.
- `surface-elevated`: `#171f2e` — Hovered containers, dropdown flyouts, and modal panels.
- `surface-highlight`: `#1f2b3e` — Segmented controls, inactive chips, and input wells.
- `border-subtle`: `#222d3d` — Structural separation borders.
- `border-active`: `#354761` — Interactive and card focus borders.
- `border-glow`: `rgba(187, 242, 70, 0.4)` — Active focus rings and verified card borders.

### Text Contrast
- `text-primary`: `#f1f5f9` — High-clarity hardware titles, prices, and specs.
- `text-secondary`: `#94a3b8` — Metadata, seller handles, and peripheral labels.
- `text-inverse`: `#0a0d14` — Bold text stamped on electric lime buttons.

## Typography

The type stack is anchored by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, distinct numerals for dense computing specs, and seamless pairing with contemporary Thai typefaces (such as Prompt or Kanit).

### Hierarchy Rules
- **Numerical Prominence:** Hardware prices, frame-rate ratings, and benchmark scores leverage `700` and `800` weights for instant comprehension. Currency glyphs (฿) scale with font metrics.
- **Thai-Latin Symbiosis:** Thai body copy sits at `body-md` (14px) and `body-lg` (16px) with relaxed line heights (`1.5` minimum) to comfortably accommodate complex tone marks and loopless characters.
- **Badges & Telemetry Labels:** Status chips, verified inspection pills, and warranty labels utilize uppercase tracking (`tracking-wide`, +0.025em to +0.05em) in `label-sm` and `label-md`.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface is structured on an 8pt architectural rhythm, maximizing usable viewport area for data-dense marketplace inventories while preserving breathing room around flagship inspection showcases.

### Layout Matrix
- **Desktop (1280px - 1440px max):** 12-column fluid grid, 20px gutters (`gutter: 1.25rem`), and 32px canvas margins (`margin: 2rem`). Catalog grids use 4 to 5 equal columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 16px gutters, and 24px margins. Cards collapse to 2 or 3 per row.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 12px gutters (`gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`), and 16px lateral padding (`margin-mobile: 1rem`). Listing cards render in a balanced 2-column arrangement.

### Component Spacing Defaults
- **Card Internals:** 12px (`space-sm` to `space-md`) internal padding around hardware imagery and metadata blocks to preserve tight gaming-grid density.
- **Section Breaks:** 40px (`space-xl`) between categorized marketplace shelves and instructional banners.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through tonal layering and luminescence rather than heavy drop shadows. Glowing halos represent energy, functional health, and system inspection validation.

### Layering Model
- **Ground Floor (`#0a0d14`):** Canvas backdrop, global navigation rail, and baseline footer.
- **Level 1 (`#111622`):** Base product containers, filter sidebars, and trust pillars. Enclosed by subtle 1px border (`#222d3d`).
- **Level 2 (`#171f2e`):** Popovers, active search modals, floating sticky action bars, and inspection verification accordions.
- **Level 3 (Neon Bloom):** Interactive highlights and verified items emit an ambient electric diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(187, 242, 70, 0.22)`.
- **Card Hover Elevation:** Cards shift slightly upward (`translateY(-2px)`) with border transformation from `#222d3d` to `#354761`, accompanied by an edge glow.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp industrial hardware edges with modern ergonomic curves. Base radius is set to **Level 2 (Rounded)**:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Product Cards, Buttons):** `rounded-md` (8px / 0.5rem) ensures an engineered, mechanical contour.
- **Badges, Filter Chips & Quick-Search Pills:** `rounded-full` (9999px) for tactile filtering, condition status tags, and specification bubbles.
- **Hero & Promotional Banners:** `rounded-xl` (16px / 1rem) to frame expansive hardware visuals with clean boundary clipping.
- **Accent Indicator:** Category and section headers feature an angled neon slash or capsule bullet to denote high-tech telemetry.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Buy Now / Inspect):** Solid electric lime (`#bbf246`), dark text (`#0a0d14`), `fontWeight: 700`, `rounded-md`. Hover triggers an outward ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(187, 242, 70, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action (Add to Cart / Specs):** Elevated surface (`#171f2e`), 1px border (`#273142`), light text (`#f1f5f9`). Hover shifts border to `#bbf246` with primary icon tint.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#94a3b8` text, transitioning to `#bbf246` with subtle lime underline on hover.

### 2. Condition & Inspection Chips
- **Condition Tag ('Used สภาพดีมาก' / 'Used สภาพดี'):** Enclosed pill chip, translucent emerald backdrop (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.15)`), crisp 1px border (`#22c55e`), high-legibility green label.
- **Filter Chips:** Capsule pills. Inactive states feature `#171f2e` surface with `#94a3b8` text. Active state renders solid `#bbf246` with dark typography.

### 3. Product Cards
- **Structure:** Dark slate container (`#111622`), 1px structural outline (`#222d3d`), 8px border radius.
- **Media Area:** High-contrast hardware render positioned over subtle radial glow, top-left pinned condition badge, top-right wishlist heart icon.
- **Data Block:** Title (2 lines clamp, `title-md`), spec row (e.g. RAM, GPU series, condition score), bold price block (current price in `#f1f5f9`, strikethrough original price in `#64748b`, and discount badge in `#22c55e` or `#facc15`).
- **Footer Controls:** Split button combo: compact cart icon button paired with an elongated high-contrast CTA button.

### 4. Search Bar & Query Input
- **Hero Search Bar:** Deep background (`#131722`), illuminated by a 1px interactive ring (`#354761`). Accompanied by quick-launch chip queries directly beneath (`Notebook Gaming`, `RTX 40 Series`, `Monitor 144Hz`).
- **Inspection Search Mode:** Integrated selector allowing switching between "Search Store" and "Verify Report ID".

### 5. Step-by-Step Trust Cards
- Four-phase workflow cards (`#111622`) displaying circular numbered neon indicators (1 to 4), illuminated tactical vector icons, high-clarity Thai action titles, and secondary reassurance copy.
