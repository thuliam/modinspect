# Product

ModInspect helps Thai buyers and sellers check used-PC component price ranges and condition context before trading.

Current public prototype:

- Home, Price Index, Product Detail, Deal Checker, Methodology.
- Compare, Build, Seller Price Advisor, Seller, Local Match, Shop/B2B, and Admin prototype screens.
- Seeded data is sample/reference data, not a live market feed.

Near-term goal: prove a data-feasibility POC where automation gathers candidate evidence and the owner reviews exceptions instead of manually researching every product.

Approved future concept: Community Data Feedback Loop. Users may report stale prices, lower/higher observed prices, product/spec mistakes, missing condition/warranty context, invalid listings, or sold/final-price evidence. These reports are evidence/signals, not votes, and cannot directly change published market prices.

Roadmap placement: Phase 4.5, after the Public Read-Only MVP and before deeper user-contribution expansion.

Deferred: Community Data Feedback Loop implementation, escrow, payments, chat, seller ratings, large B2B dashboards, vector/RAG systems, and paid live-provider automation.
